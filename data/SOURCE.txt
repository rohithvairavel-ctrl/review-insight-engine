Dataset sources (public):

1) SNAP Stanford — Amazon Product Reviews (Digital Music 5-core)
   http://snap.stanford.edu/data/amazon/productGraph.html
   McAuley et al. (Image-based recommendations / visual evolution of fashion trends).

2) UCI ML Repository — Sentiment Labelled Sentences (Amazon cells, Yelp, IMDB)
   https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/331/sentiment+labelled+sentences
   Kotzias et al., KDD 2015.

Committed: data/reviews_sample.csv (stratified subset for git).
Full local set: data/reviews_full.csv via `python scripts/download_data.py` (gitignored).

Star mapping: 1-2=negative, 3=neutral, 4-5=positive.
